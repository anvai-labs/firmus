# Legal and Ethical Use Disclaimer

## Important Notice

**FIRMUS is a research tool for legitimate system administration and authorized
monitoring purposes only. Unauthorized access to computer systems is illegal
in most jurisdictions.**

---

## Intended Use Cases

FIRMUS is designed for the following legitimate purposes:

### ✅ Authorized Use

1. **Personal Device Management**
   - Monitoring your own IoT devices, home servers, and computers
   - Managing your own network infrastructure
   - Accessing your own systems remotely

2. **Organizational Administration**
   - Managing organizational assets with proper authorization
   - System administration duties as part of employment
   - Incident response with explicit written authorization

3. **Academic Research**
   - Research projects with Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval
   - Educational demonstrations in controlled environments
   - Security research with proper permissions and disclosures

4. **Consulting and Professional Services**
   - Managed service provider (MSP) activities with client contracts
   - Professional IT services with documented authorization
   - Penetration testing with signed engagement letters

---

## Prohibited Use

### ❌ Unauthorized Use Is Illegal

Using FIRMUS for any of the following is **strictly prohibited**:

1. **Unauthorized Access**
   - Accessing systems you don't own or administer
   - Bypassing security controls without permission
   - Monitoring systems without owner consent

2. **Violating Laws**
   - Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA) - USA
   - Computer Misuse Act - UK
   - EU Cybercrime Directive
   - Similar computer crime laws in other jurisdictions

3. **Privacy Violations**
   - Accessing private data without consent
   - Monitoring individuals without authorization
   - Intercepting communications illegally

---

## Legal Requirements by Jurisdiction

### United States

**Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)**
- Unauthorized access to computer systems is a federal crime
- Penalties: Fines and up to 10 years imprisonment
- Civil liability available to victims

**State Laws**
- Many states have additional computer crime laws
- California: Penal Code 502
- New York: Penal Code 156.05

### United Kingdom

**Computer Misuse Act 1990**
- Unauthorized access is a criminal offense
- Penalties: Fines and up to 2 years imprisonment (Section 1)
- Up to 5 years for unauthorized access with intent (Section 2)

### European Union

**EU Cybercrime Directive**
- Member states have implemented computer crime laws
- Cross-border prosecution possible
- GDPR implications for personal data

### Other Jurisdictions

Most countries have similar laws prohibiting unauthorized computer access.
**Research your local laws before deployment.**

## Authorization Requirements

### Written Authorization Required

Before deploying FIRMUS, obtain:

1. **For Personal Use**
   - Ownership of the target systems
   - Authority to configure network devices

2. **For Organizational Use**
   - Written authorization from system owner
   - Documentation of business need
   - Approval from appropriate authority (IT director, CISO, etc.)

3. **For Research**
   - IRB approval for human subjects research
   - Written consent from participants
   - Approval from institution's legal/compliance team

4. **For Consulting**
   - Signed contract with client
   - Scope of work clearly defined
   - Liability and indemnification provisions

## Best Practices for Compliance

### Before Deployment

1. **Document Authorization**
   - Keep written permission on file
   - Note scope and duration
   - Maintain contact information

2. **Notify Stakeholders**
   - Inform IT security teams
   - Notify system users
   - Post monitoring notices if required

3. **Review Policies**
   - Organizational acceptable use policy
   - Industry regulations (HIPAA, PCI-DSS, etc.)
   - Union agreements or employment contracts

### During Operation

1. **Principle of Least Privilege**
   - Monitor only necessary directories
   - Use read-only mode when possible
   - Limit agent capabilities to minimum required

2. **Data Protection**
   - Encrypt collected data
   - Secure storage of downloaded files
   - Implement data retention policies

3. **Transparency**
   - Log all monitoring activities
   - Provide audit trail
   - Allow subject access requests (if applicable)

### After Completion

1. **Clean Up**
   - Remove agents from systems
   - Delete collected data per policy
   - Document completion

2. **Documentation**
   - Summary of activities
   - Data collected and disposition
   - Any incidents or issues
